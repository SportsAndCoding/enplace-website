⚠️ Preliminary Document

This privacy policy is a working document pending formal legal review. It reflects En Place's current data practices and privacy commitments.

This Privacy Policy describes the personal data we collect and process to provide products and services offered by En Place, Inc. ("En Place," "we," "us," or "our"), including our web-based platform, mobile applications, staff management tools, mood tracking features, and related services (collectively, "En Place Products and Services" or "Services").

En Place is a restaurant emotional intelligence platform designed to help reduce staff turnover through mood tracking and management insights. We are committed to protecting the privacy of restaurant operators, managers, and staff members who use our Services.

1. What Personal Data Do We Collect?

Personal data is any information from or about an identified or identifiable person. We may collect the following categories of personal data when you use or interact with En Place Products and Services:

Account Information

Information associated with a restaurant or organization account, which may include: restaurant name, business address, administrator contact information, account ID, billing and payment information, subscription plan details, and account settings.

Profile and User Information

Information associated with manager and staff profiles, which may include: name, display name, email address, phone number, job title or position, hire date, employment status, wage information, and profile photos.

Mood and Wellness Data

Information submitted through our Staff Stability Engine (SSE), including daily mood check-ins, wellness indicators, and emotional state data.

CRITICAL: Mood data is collected anonymously and is never directly associated with individual staff identities in management-facing reports. This anonymization is fundamental to maintaining staff trust and the integrity of our emotional intelligence platform.

Schedule and Shift Data

Information related to work schedules, including: shift times and dates, availability preferences, time-off requests, call-in records, shift coverage requests, overtime records, and scheduling preferences.

Communication Data

Information from communications through our platform, including: SMS notifications, in-app messages, shift coverage requests and responses, and notification preferences.

Device Information

Information about devices used to access our Services, which may include: device type, operating system, browser type, IP address (which may be used to infer general location), and device identifiers.

Usage Information

Information about how users interact with our Services, including: feature usage patterns, login times, pages viewed, actions taken within the platform, and performance data.

Recruiting and Candidate Data (Stable Hire)

For restaurants using our Stable Hire recruiting features: candidate names, contact information, resume data, application responses, assessment results, interview notes, and hiring pipeline status.

2. How Do We Use Personal Data?

En Place uses personal data to provide, improve, and secure our Services. We do not sell personal data to third parties. Specifically, we use personal data for the following purposes:

Provide En Place Products and Services

To provide our platform to restaurant accounts and their authorized users, including: processing mood check-ins, generating anonymized management insights, facilitating schedule management, sending shift notifications, enabling staff communication, processing payments, and providing customer support.

Generate Anonymized Insights

To analyze mood and wellness data in aggregate to generate management insights, identify trends, and provide early warning indicators of potential retention issues. Individual staff members are never identified in these reports except where their data is already publicly observable (such as scheduled shifts).

Product Development and Improvement

To develop, test, and improve our products and services, including using aggregated and anonymized data to train and improve our predictive models and algorithms.

Security and Fraud Prevention

To authenticate accounts, detect and prevent malicious activity, address security threats, and protect the integrity of our Services.

Communications

To communicate with you about your account, service updates, shift notifications, coverage requests, and changes to our policies. We may also send promotional communications with your consent, which you can opt out of at any time.

Legal Compliance

To comply with applicable laws, respond to valid legal process, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect our rights and the rights of our users.

3. Consent and Opt-In

En Place is built on trust. We obtain your explicit consent before collecting or processing personal data. We do not collect data without your knowledge and affirmative agreement.

Staff Portal Consent

When staff members first access the En Place Staff Portal, they are presented with clear information about what data will be collected and how it will be used. Staff must affirmatively agree to these terms before using the platform. This includes:

  • Consent to submit mood check-ins (which are processed anonymously)
  • Consent to receive shift-related notifications
  • Acknowledgment of how their schedule data will be used

Staff participation in mood check-ins is voluntary. Staff may use scheduling features without participating in mood tracking.

SMS and Text Message Consent

En Place uses SMS text messaging to deliver shift notifications, coverage requests, and other time-sensitive communications. We will never send you text messages without your explicit opt-in consent.

TCPA Compliance: In accordance with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), you must affirmatively opt in to receive SMS communications from En Place. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.

By providing your mobile phone number and agreeing to receive SMS notifications, you consent to receive text messages from En Place related to:

  • Shift assignments and schedule changes
  • Open shift opportunities and coverage requests
  • Time-sensitive operational alerts
  • Account and security notifications

Message frequency varies based on restaurant activity. Message and data rates may apply. See our Terms of Service for complete SMS terms.

Marketing Communications

We will only send you promotional or marketing communications if you have explicitly opted in to receive them. Marketing consent is separate from operational communications (like shift notifications). You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by:

  • Clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any marketing email
  • Updating your preferences in your account settings
  • Contacting us at support@en-place.ai

Opting out of marketing communications will not affect operational messages necessary to provide our Services.

Withdrawing Consent

You may withdraw your consent at any time. Depending on the type of consent:

  • SMS: Reply STOP to any message
  • Marketing emails: Click unsubscribe or update account settings
  • Mood check-ins: Simply stop submitting them (participation is always voluntary)
  • Platform use: Contact your manager or our support team to deactivate your account

Withdrawing consent may limit your ability to use certain features of the Services. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal.

Manager and Account Owner Consent

Restaurant managers and account owners consent to our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy when they create an account or are granted access by an account owner. By subscribing to En Place, account owners confirm they have authority to enroll their restaurant and staff in the platform.

4. How Do We Share Personal Data?

En Place does not sell personal data. We share personal data only in the following circumstances:

Service Providers

We work with third-party service providers to operate and improve our Services. These providers are contractually bound to protect personal data and use it only for the purposes we specify. Our key service providers include:

Individual mood check-in data is never shared with managers in an identifiable form.

Legal Requirements

We may share personal data when required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or prominent notice on our website of any change in ownership or uses of your personal data.

Aggregated and Anonymized Data

We may share aggregated, anonymized data that cannot reasonably be used to identify individuals for research, benchmarking, and industry analysis purposes.

5. Anonymization and Staff Privacy

En Place is built on a foundation of staff trust. Our anonymization principles are central to this commitment:

Mood-Based Data: Always Anonymous

Mood check-ins and emotional wellness data submitted by staff are processed anonymously. When mood-based insights are escalated to management, they appear as "Multiple staff affected" or similar aggregate descriptions. Individual staff members are never identified based on their mood submissions.

Schedule-Based Data: Observable Information

Schedule-based events (such as call-ins, shift coverage, or attendance) may identify individual staff members because this information is inherently observable and part of normal restaurant operations.

The Distinction Matters

This distinction ensures that staff can provide honest mood feedback without fear of identification, while managers still receive actionable insights about publicly observable scheduling matters. We believe this balance is essential for building genuine trust and achieving the retention benefits our platform provides.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using TLS/SSL
  • Encryption of sensitive data at rest
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring data isolation between restaurant accounts
  • JWT-based authentication with secure token management
  • Regular security assessments and monitoring
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel
  • Audit logging of management actions

While we strive to protect your personal data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our Services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Our retention periods are determined by:

  • The duration of your relationship with En Place
  • Legal obligations requiring retention of certain records
  • Legitimate business purposes such as maintaining audit trails and resolving disputes
  • Your requests for data deletion (subject to legal requirements)

When personal data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymize it in accordance with our data retention policies.
